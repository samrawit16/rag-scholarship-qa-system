Who can apply for a Chevening SIDS Scholarship?
The Chevening SIDS Scholarship is an opportunity for outstanding individuals from Small Island Developing States (SIDS) to pursue a one-year master’s degree in the UK.
To be eligible for a Chevening SIDS Scholarship, you must meet
the standard eligibility criteria for Chevening
, and be a citizen from one of the participating countries shown on the map below.
