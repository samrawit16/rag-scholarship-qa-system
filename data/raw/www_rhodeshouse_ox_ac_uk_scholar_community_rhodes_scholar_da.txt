Rhodes Scholar Database
Since 1903, Rhodes Scholars have been selected from constituencies around the world to come to study in the University of Oxford. We find, bring together and develop exceptional people who are impatient with the way things are and have the courage to act.
'Search database' to find any Rhodes Scholar by First Name, Last Name, Region, College and Year.
For example, if you are looking for John Smith, please type in 'John' and the database will show you every Rhodes Scholar with that first name. Or you can type 'India' into the region field to find all Rhodes Scholars selected from India. If you are looking for your classmates, put in your class year and their names will appear.
