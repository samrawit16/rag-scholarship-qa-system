Why study in the UK?
Studying in the UK with Chevening is about more than earning a world-class degree. It’s a chance to experience UK culture, meet people from different backgrounds, and build connections that can last a lifetime.
In this section
The UK is home to some of the world’s leading universities, but there’s so much more to discover beyond the classroom. From historic landmarks and vibrant cities to music, festivals, museums and world-class sporting events, studying here gives you the opportunity to experience the culture, creativity and traditions that make the UK unique.
As a Chevening scholar, you’ll join a global community of people who are passionate about making a difference. You’ll develop your knowledge and skills, exchange ideas with people from around the world, and build networks that can support you long after your studies end.
A Chevening Scholarship is more than a degree. It’s an opportunity to experience the UK, broaden your perspective and become part of a global network working together to address some of the world’s most pressing challenges.
Nature
Explore the UK's natural beauty
The British countryside offers beautiful landscapes and scenery. From mountains and sandy beaches to lakes and waterfalls, it’s the perfect place if you love an outdoor adventure!

Community
Build connections and networks
Studying in the UK through Chevening gives you the opportunity to build international friendships.
Hear from Chevening scholars
on how they connected with others from around the world and made lifelong connections.
Eating out
Eat like a local
The UK has a diverse food scene with something for everyone. Try dishes from all UK nations, whether it’s haggis, afternoon tea, or a Sunday roast, our scholars have tasted it all.
Hear about their favourites!
Music and art
Discover the UK's creative side
The UK offers rich creative experiences, from historic museums and galleries to incredible street art. Experience world-famous festivals like the Edinburgh Fringe and attend free concerts.
Check out events for scholars!
Public transport
Travel easily and explore
The UK has a great network of buses and railways, giving you the opportunity to explore and embrace everything the UK has to offer.
Check out how
Shantal travelled to Scotland
during her Chevening scholarship.
History and heritage
Step back in time
Enjoy the rich history of the UK. Walk in the footsteps of famous writers like Shakespeare and explore castles that kings and queens once lived in!
Check out the history locations on our
bucket list.
Sport
Get in the game
The UK is a dream for any sports lover. It has world-class facilities, a range of sports clubs and iconic events like Wimbledon.
The home of rowing, cricket and golf, there’s lots of ways to get involved! Chevening scholar Grace
shares her experience
of doing a master’s in Sports Coaching.
Language skills
Master the English language
Improve your English skills
through daily interactions and in the classroom, making you more fluent and confident in the language.
The English language opens a door, connecting you with others around the world.

Chevening's vision
Want to put your language skills to the test?
British slang is full of fun expressions that add colour to everyday conversations. Studying in the UK immerses you in this unique language, helping you master English. Ready to test your knowledge?
